# Clareira

A refined desktop concept for Alínea. The name describes an opening that gives the writing room to breathe. The warm ivory field, green accents, serif manuscript and floating tools continue the issue-comment direction the user liked.

Open `edit.html`, or use the Clareira section in the parent gallery. These are five static HTML/CSS boards; navigation links connect them for inspection. Buttons do not edit, generate or save documents. Screenshots reflect the 1440 × 960 boards.

- Writing: full manuscript field; outline and document properties collapsed; insertion and focus reachable from a compact island. Formatting appears above selected text without reserving extra space when selected.
- Review: one opened floating tray with passage context, comment navigation, reply and resolution, preserved comments with uncertain anchors, and optional writing suggestions.
- Templates: two locally available article paths; planned report/thesis types remain unavailable. Metadata compatibility and the missing SBC abstract are visible before changing formats.
- Export: source checks, chosen destination, pinned model and the earlier PDF count precede generation. Writing suggestions are separate from structural validation.
- Preview: an updated first-page illustration with matching pagination; one save action and an explicit route back to writing.

Visibility follows DESIGN.md: document and structure entry points recover hidden inspectors; secondary details stay collapsed; each open island has a focused purpose. Native window decorations are left to the host. No permanent sidebar, ribbon or simulated paper in writing mode. At narrower desktop widths, prose and trays adjust without becoming a website layout.

All manuscript content, identities, timestamps, counts, versions and build states are illustrative. No standards compliance, working PDF generation, or implemented accessibility is claimed. Relevant product specs remain draft. PRODUCT.md and DESIGN.md were preserved.

The expanded article gallery includes ABNT, SBC, IEEE, APA 7, ACM and Elsevier. International entries are planned catalog studies with illustrative thumbnails; publisher/event variants and compatibility remain unverified. Only the two original local article paths are depicted as available in the concept.
