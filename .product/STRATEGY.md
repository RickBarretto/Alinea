# Product Strategy

## Vision

Enable academic authors to focus on research and arguments while versioned templates handle publishing mechanics. [PRODUCT.md](../PRODUCT.md) is the durable product record; this file summarizes it for product.md tooling.

## Target User

An academic author preparing an submission who needs a reliable PDF without learning Markdown syntax, LaTeX, or manual formatting. Students, researchers, and professors are confirmed audiences. A narrower persona has not been selected.

## Positioning

For academic authors who need approachable writing and ownership of their files, Alínea is a local-first native desktop editor combining semantic Markdown editing with a versioned academic publishing pipeline.

## Success Criteria

An author unfamiliar with Markdown can create, write, review, and export an article offline. Metadata, citations, captions, cross-references, and actual page counts are handled through the application. Files remain independently editable, and representative ABNT and SBC exports pass baseline template regression checks.

## North Star Metric

A numeric metric and target have not been selected. Usability criteria do not establish measured adoption or validated customer outcomes.

## Scope

Windows, macOS, and Linux desktop. Brazilian academic templates first; later work expands analysis, template packages, and publishing workflows. See the [roadmap](ROADMAP.md).
