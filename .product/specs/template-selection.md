# Template Selection

## User story

As an academic author, I want to choose the academic template for my document, so that I can produce the appropriate article without manually setting layout rules.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The author can select an ABNT scientific article or SBC conference paper template for a project and see the current selection before export.
- [ ] The initial catalog distinguishes ABNT scientific article, academic assignment or report, thesis and dissertation, and SBC conference paper as separate template types; availability is clear.
- [ ] The article templates are the first proven export path; unavailable types are not represented as ready for use.
- [ ] Changing the selection preserves compatible metadata and identifies fields or document content needing attention.
- [ ] The project records the chosen template and its version so reopening and re-exporting does not silently select a newer version.
- [ ] The author can see the applicable template requirements and any known compatibility limits before relying on the output.

## Constraints

Template-controlled formatting replaces manual margin and typography configuration. Do not claim official compliance without validation. Initial templates must permit institution-specific requirements. Public authoring and management of institutional variants and the plugin ecosystem are later work.
